# Chapter 9 Lab: Guardrails, Observability, and Scaling Multi-Agent Workloads

**Estimated time:** 30 minutes

## Exercise

Add NeMo Guardrails topical and safety rails in front of a deployed multi-agent workflow, then instrument step-level tracing to identify which agent step caused an intermittent, non-deterministic failure

## Key takeaway

Guardrails must be applied at both input and output, and step-level tracing — not repeated re-runs — is the reliable way to debug non-deterministic agent failures at scale.
