# Chapter 6 Lab: Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Agents

**Estimated time:** 35 minutes

## Exercise

Build a hybrid retrieval pipeline (dense embeddings via NVIDIA NeMo Retriever + keyword search) with a re-ranking stage, and feed the top-k grounded chunks into an agent's reasoning step

## Key takeaway

Use RAG with hybrid retrieval and re-ranking for large or changing knowledge bases; reserve long-context for small, static, tightly-scoped inputs.
