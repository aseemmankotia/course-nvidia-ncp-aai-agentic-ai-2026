# Chapter 12 Lab: Full Exam Simulation and Time-Management Strategy

**Estimated time:** 120 minutes

## Exercise

Complete a timed, 65-question full-length practice exam simulation covering all six domains, then review a domain-by-domain score breakdown to identify weak areas

## Key takeaway

Manage the 120-minute/60-70-question budget with a strict per-question time cap, flag-and-review strategy, and disciplined elimination against the exact superlative each scenario question asks for.
